VC Pack - VST3 for FL Studio (Windows, 64-bit)

1. Copy every "VC ... .vst3" folder from this archive into
   C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3
2. In FL Studio: Options > Manage plugins > Find installed plugins (Start scan).
3. The effects appear under "VC Audio":
   VC VintageVerb, VC Room, VC Plate, VC Shimmer, VC Supermassive,
   VC Delay, VC Freq Echo, VC Space Mod, VC UberMod.

Every parameter, including the Mode selector, is automatable in FL Studio.
